【中文 About】
--------------------------------------------------------------
小數據所是一人工作室。設計、開發、商店文案、回信，都是同一個人。

會做這幾個 app，是從一個滿生活的念頭開始的：有些你很在意的事，從來沒有人幫你算過。

銀行幫你算錢，公司幫你算工時，平台幫你算你看過哪些廣告。至於你今年走進幾次 live house、你的晚上實際上給了哪幾個人、一年一次的那天究竟省下多少，算出來其實很有意思，可惜對別人沒什麼用處，就一直空在那裡。

我做的就是把這幾格空白填起來。

目前只有壽星日真的上架，Bandary 的 Android 版和 RelateStats 還在封測。固定要付的就是網域、主機和 Google Play 開發者帳號，金額都不大，我自己付了一陣子了。有人一起分擔的話，我就能把心思多放在把東西做完上面。

【English About】
--------------------------------------------------------------
Small Data Co. is a one-person studio. The design, the code, the store listings, the replies to your email — same person throughout.

These apps started from a fairly ordinary thought: some of the things you care about have never been counted by anyone.

Your bank keeps count of your money. Your employer keeps count of your hours. Platforms keep count of the ads you sat through. Then there is everything else — how many gigs you made it to this year, which handful of people your evenings went to, what you saved on the one day a year that belongs to you. All interesting to know, all worth nothing to anybody else, so it sits there uncounted.

Filling in those few blanks is the whole job.

Everything the apps work out stays on your own phone. No uploads, no accounts, no server.

Only Shouxingri is properly on the store so far; Bandary's Android build and RelateStats are still in closed testing. The running costs are domains, hosting and the Google Play developer account — small amounts I have been covering for a while. A hand with them means more of my attention goes into getting the apps finished.
